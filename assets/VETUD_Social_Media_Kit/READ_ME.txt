FORMATS
PNG exports at the exact pixel dimensions in each filename. JPG copies of photo-based artwork are included for smaller uploads. Profile pictures have solid backgrounds. Logo masters have genuine transparency.
SVG logo/profile files contain vector paths and gradients with no font dependencies. SVG social backgrounds contain a compressed 1280 x 854 raster alpine image with vector logo overlays: they are editable compositions, not fully vector mountain illustrations. Larger backgrounds resample this raster artwork. The original generated alpine image is separately available in the conversation.
