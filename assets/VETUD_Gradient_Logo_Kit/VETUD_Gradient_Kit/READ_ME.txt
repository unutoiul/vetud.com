VETUD GRADIENT LOGO KIT

Approved angular two-node symbol, rebuilt as clean vectors with smooth navy-blue and silver-blue gradients. Thin outlined VETUD lettering and PEPTIDE SERIES.

24 SVG files and 24 PNG files in the full kit. Three layouts: stacked, horizontal, symbol. Eight colour variants including true transparent, light, dark and one-colour versions.

SVG: editable paths, circles and gradient fills. No embedded raster images and no font dependencies. Open in Illustrator, Affinity Designer or Inkscape.
PNG sizes: stacked 3936 pixels wide; horizontal 4800 pixels wide; symbol 2400 x 2400 pixels.

RGB colours. Have a printer perform a colour-managed CMYK conversion for printing. The typography is a path reconstruction of the supplied logo, not a supplied original font file.

Do not distort aspect ratios. Use white/reverse versions on dark backgrounds. At very small sizes use the symbol alone.
