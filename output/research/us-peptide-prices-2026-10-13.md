## Selection and implementation

- Semaglutide 10/20 mg use the mid-to-upper vial retail comparisons as a starting point for a packaged kit. The 40 mg price is explicitly estimated.
- Tirzepatide uses rounded starting prices near the Nextday and US Peptide Labs listings, with a modest kit premium on the larger amounts.
- Retatrutide 10/20 mg are near the observed retail benchmarks. The selected 40 mg price includes a modest premium over the lower vial benchmarks and remains below the highest vial benchmark.
- Each product has 10 mg, 20 mg and 40 mg variants. The original two images are reused; descriptions state that the pictures show 40 mg packaging.
- Existing 40 mg variant IDs, image URLs, stock and publication statuses are retained. New stock is unset and backorders are disabled. Semaglutide and Tirzepatide remain drafts. Existing non-USD prices are retained; new variants are priced only in USD.
- Product metadata records the research date, sources, rationale and one-time pricing revision. Rerunning the script does not duplicate variants or reset later manual price edits.
