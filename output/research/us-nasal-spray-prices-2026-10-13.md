## Plain-language descriptions and ingredients

Selank: a synthetic peptide, explained as a short chain of amino acids, investigated for stress-related signaling and communication between nerve cells. Research context is supported by [Selank Administration Affects the Expression of Some Genes Involved in GABAergic Neurotransmission](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26924987/), a rat study. This does not substantiate a clinical benefit for VETUD's product.

Semax: a synthetic peptide, explained as a short chain of amino acids, investigated for nerve-cell signaling and proteins involved in nerve-cell growth and maintenance. Research context is supported by [Comparison of the temporary dynamics of NGF and BDNF gene expression in rat hippocampus, frontal cortex, and retina under Semax action](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19662538/). This is animal research, not proof of a consumer benefit.
