# Article reading design

Use the existing VETUD identity: navy #15265e, ink #192b4a, blue #3268aa,
porcelain #fbfcfe, pale blue #f3f7fc and divider #e2e8f0. Keep Manrope.

Lead with the question the reader came to answer, the plain-language summary,
source-based status and a relevant illustration. Keep the illustration caption-free;
identify conceptual artwork in its alternative text and retain chemical-source links.

Desktop: a quiet 210px contents rail beside a 740px reading column. The article
title and artwork share a balanced hero above it. Mobile: one column, with an
expandable contents menu and comfortable paragraph spacing. No text overlays.

Use 17–18px, high-contrast prose, about 65–75 characters per line, line-height 1.8.
Evidence summaries should be compact and should explain the measured outcome.
No oversized animal-data label. Separate the educational article from related
videos, sources, further reading and the existing live research catalog.

Review: preserve the evidence and uncertainty, remove decorative headline accents,
make all navigation real links, and keep the initial article server-rendered.
Use Article/BreadcrumbList JSON-LD, descriptive social cards and preferred URLs
for the identical English content across regional storefronts. No invented authors,
review credentials, freshness dates or ranking claims.
