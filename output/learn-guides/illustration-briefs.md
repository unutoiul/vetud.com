# Vetud Learn illustration briefs

The built-in ImageGen tool produced each cover individually. The guide cards and article pages share the corresponding WebP file.

## Survodutide

Generate a standalone landscape 3:2 editorial cover for Vetud's science magazine. Contemporary scientific still life, physically crafted museum object, art-directed studio photograph, pleasing subtle surface texture, uncluttered matte pale mineral background, soft shadows, restrained color palette with a single high-contrast accent. Compose the subject in the central 65% with generous breathing room above and below for wide card crops. No text, letters, logos, labels, frames, labels, charts with numbers, or packaging. A conceptual illustration, not a literal molecular structure. Subject: A hand-sized translucent amber liver specimen, its two lobes visibly sculpted in wax, cradled at a junction between two delicate colored signal threads. Warm brass and ultramarine.
