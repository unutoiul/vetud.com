Tesamorelin

Also known as Egrifta

A growth-hormone-releasing medicine studied for changes in visceral fat, the deep fat around the organs.

Approved medicine

What it is

Tesamorelin is a stabilized copy of growth-hormone-releasing hormone (GHRH).

How it works

It raises the body’s own growth hormone, which helps break down visceral fat, the deep fat packed around the organs.

What the research found

  • In a 26-week trial of 412 adults with lipodystrophy and excess abdominal fat, visceral fat fell by 15.2% on tesamorelin and rose by 5.0% on placebo.
  • Triglycerides, a type of fat in the blood, also improved.
  • These results apply to the specific patient group studied and do not establish effectiveness for weight loss in the general population.

Known risks and side effects

  • More people on tesamorelin stopped because of side effects than on placebo.
  • Growth hormone can affect blood sugar, so this is monitored.

Is it approved?

Approved medicine

A medicines regulator, such as the FDA in the US or the EMA in Europe, has approved it for specific uses.

Egrifta is approved in the US for a narrowly defined prescription indication involving excess abdominal fat. It is not approved for general weight-loss management.
