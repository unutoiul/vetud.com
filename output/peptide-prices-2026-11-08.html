How prices were calculated

  • Arithmetic mean of sampled advertised single-item prices for the same compound, total amount and format, converted to USD as quoted currency ÷ units of that currency per USD. One listing per supplier per amount and format. No affiliate codes, paid membership discounts, subscriptions or multi-vial wholesale prices.
  • Prices exclude delivery; VAT/tax remains included wherever a supplier includes it in its displayed price. Purity, sterility, salt form and lot identity were not independently verified. Product-label matches do not establish analytical equivalence.
  • Regular prices are rounded to the nearest whole USD from the cent-rounded benchmark. Other currencies are converted using the store's currency source and rounded to the currency's smallest unit. Black Friday sale prices are half the rounded regular price, rounded to the same unit.
  • Dual-chamber vials and full kits have no verified identical international package. Their prices are estimates: vial benchmark plus the pre-existing VETUD packaging premium. The full-kit increment above the dual-chamber cartridge remains $40. No manufacturing, testing or fulfilment cost data were supplied, so these are market-based prices, not profit forecasts.
  • ARA-290 20mg is estimated from 16mg listings by multiplying their mean by 20/16. The 10-syringe box is estimated pro rata from a 20-pack. The water cartridge retains its existing $28 regular price because no compatible dual-chamber refill comparison was found.
  • Nasal sprays are separate from powder vials. Unverified bottle fill volumes are flagged as format estimates. Single-quote benchmarks are marked †; they are single advertised prices, not a multi-vendor average.
  • Excluded closed suppliers (Peptide Sciences and UK Peptides), our own VETUD prices, known out-of-stock source variants, N-acetyl substitutes, tablets, mixed nasal products, and Core's explicitly full-length TB4 listings when the VETUD description specifies the fragment. Blend label ratios are retained but not independently tested.
