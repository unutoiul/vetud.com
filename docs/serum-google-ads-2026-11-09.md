# Anua serum Google Ads campaign (UK test)

Draft of 9 November 2026. Nothing has been created in Google Ads and no money has been spent.

## Before you turn it on

1. **The serums must be live.** On 9 November they were not in the live store, so the backend has to be deployed first.
2. **Decide the delivery price.** Delivery is $24.95 (about £18.65) on a serum of $25 (about £18.70), so about £37 in total. Boots and John Lewis listed the niacinamide serum at £19.80.
3. **Add sales tracking.** The site has no Google Ads tag yet, so you could not see which clicks became orders. UK and EU visitors must agree to ad cookies first.
4. **Check the stock is genuine Anua.** The ads use the Anua name and must lead to the real product.
5. **Expect a possible rejection.** Google also looks at the site the ad leads to, and this site sells peptides. Do not hide the peptides or send Google a different page.

## Campaign settings

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign type | Search only (turn off Display Network and Search Partners) |
| Location | United Kingdom, people in that location only |
| Daily budget | $5 (about £3.75) in total for both serums |
| Bidding | Maximize clicks, with a maximum click price of £0.50 (a starting guess; adjust after a week) |
| Ad groups | One for each serum, as below |

Google can spend up to twice the daily budget on a single day, so $5 keeps the campaign inside a strict $10-a-day limit.

**Negative keywords (whole campaign):** prescription, treatment, cure, tablets, injection, peptide, dupe, review, free, sample, jobs

**Never use:** the prescription brand names Finacea or Cyklokapron (both on Google's restricted list), or claims to treat rosacea, melasma, acne or any other condition.

Add a price headline only once the live price is final, and make sure it matches the product page.
