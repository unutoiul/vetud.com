# Google Ads readiness and launch plan - 9 November 2026

User brief: a confirmed strict maximum of US$10/day total (USD is the planning assumption), a UK business/dispatch location, and Europe, UK, UAE and Saudi Arabia as target markets. This is a planning audit. No Google Ads campaigns have been created, no ad spending has been enabled by this work, and no live storefront products or prices have been changed.

The live public catalog contains 29 products and 157 variants. The country/product plan covers 34 markets and 986 combinations. Europe here means the 31 configured continental European markets; the UK is listed separately. UAE and Saudi Arabia need storefront preparation. The US, Canada and Australia are excluded from this requested advertising plan.

At this budget, the proposal is a focused UK Search test using only products whose advertising eligibility is established, rather than dividing the daily budget among every product and country. Other markets are expansion plans, not additional $10/day campaigns. Account currency, existing campaign spend, product economics and keyword costs must be read before setting bids or budgets.

Google Ads and GA4 are not connected through the available connector. The connector supports paused Search campaigns, ad groups, responsive search ads, keywords, locations and budgets. Its location action does not expose the advanced Presence-only setting; that setting needs UI/API verification. Google's September 2026 change means Search ad language is matched from creative and landing pages, rather than a campaign language checkbox. [Language policy](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/1722078?hl=en), [location options](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/1722038?hl=en).

The user confirmed: never more than US$10/day. For standard Search campaigns governed by Google's 2x daily billed-media limit, the proposed combined average daily budgets must total at most US$5 equivalent. This would average about US$152 of media spend in a full unchanged month. Account currency, taxes, surcharges, existing VETUD campaigns and the budget-day timezone must be checked before activation; a lower base budget may be necessary. A higher budget chosen earlier that day can affect that day's ceiling. Automated pausing is not the sole safeguard. No budget has been applied in Google Ads. [Spending limits](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/10486637?hl=en).

## Measurement and optimization

No GA4/Google Ads tag integration was found in the checked storefront source. The current privacy policy says advertising cookies are not used. Any tagging work needs the actual measurement/conversion IDs, a consent-aware implementation and matching privacy/cookie wording before deployment. Validate tracking against the live site as well.

The main sales objective should measure confirmed paid orders, with unique transaction IDs, order value and currency. Pending bank/crypto orders should not inflate paid-order optimization. The backend has payment-captured and order-placed event handlers that can be assessed for this purpose. Do not send health conditions or unnecessary personal information into analytics. Audience strategies must respect health-related personalization restrictions; customer-match/remarketing audiences are not assumed suitable for this catalog. [Sensitive targeting policy](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/143465?hl=en).

After the account is connected: inspect existing campaigns, policy decisions, search terms, keywords, conversions, spend and revenue; identify eligible products; propose a small UK Search test with relevant product ad groups and matching product pages; verify Presence targeting, creative language, paid-order tracking and total budget; review actual query quality, CPA, value and contribution margin before expansion. No CPC, CPA or ROAS performance is invented in this plan.
