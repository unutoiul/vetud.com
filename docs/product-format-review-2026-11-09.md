# Product format review — 9 November 2026

Reviewed all 29 live products, plus the pending Decapeptide-12 catalog entry. The owner confirmed that manufacturer sterility, endotoxin and pen/cartridge-compatibility documentation was unavailable.

The catalog decision is to withhold unsupported pen and dual-chamber formats and offer BAC-water/needle accessories only as unchecked options on eligible peptide powder vials. Published administration routes do not establish that a particular research product is suitable for injection. Sterility and formulation quality require product-specific evidence. See the [FDA ingredient-quality alert](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/human-drug-compounding/fda-highlights-concerns-using-dietary-ingredient-glutathione-compound-sterile-injectables) and [TGA safety advisory](https://www.tga.gov.au/safety/safety-monitoring-and-information/safety-alerts/understanding-your-responsibilities-when-importing-compounding-and-supplying-unapproved-peptide-products).

Database result: **99 variants soft-deleted across 28 products; 58 variants retained. All retained base-price records and currencies matched the pre-change backup.** Displayed offer prices were checked against the backend’s calculated prices, including its current sale. Product IDs and retained variant IDs were preserved. Standalone water bottles and syringes remain available as supplies.
